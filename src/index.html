From hunch to live desk

Nothing to code, nothing to fund, zero formulas to memorise. Draft it, test it, flip the switch.

  1. Stage 1

    Sketch the playbook

    Begin with a preset or type rules by hand, select a handful of coins and set a loss cap.

  2. Stage 2

    Rehearse the past

    Hourly candles run through the settings you chose, so the outcome is visible ahead of committing.

  3. Stage 3

    Switch it on

    Authorize with one wallet signature. Your desk reads the tape hourly and files its reasoning.

  4. Stage 4 Later

    Share a proven desk

    Package a desk with a verified history so the crowd can mirror it.

Reasoning on the record

A desk keeps a diary. On each pass it records the signal behind the call: entered, exited or stood aside.

  • The precise trigger: daily move, gap to the mean, band position
  • Floors and targets logged the instant each one fires
  • Plain sentences you can read back and argue with

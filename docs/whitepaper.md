# Zivilia Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Markets on Robinhood Chain never close, yet most people can only watch them. Zivilia's vision is a world where anyone can express a market view as a handful of plain rules, test it honestly against history, and let a desk carry it out while keeping a written account of every decision.

Our mission is to make rule-based trading legible. A desk should be easy to build, easy to rehearse and easy to audit, and its results should always be measured against the plainest alternative: holding.

## Principles

1. **Rules before black boxes.** Every action traces back to a rule the author wrote.
2. **Honest rehearsal.** Replays use real hourly history and the exact settings chosen.
3. **Reasoning on the record.** Each pass logs what the desk saw and why it acted or stood by.
4. **Open facts only.** Anything the project states publicly, such as links and addresses, is recorded once and rendered from that record.
